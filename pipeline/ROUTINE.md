6a. Deep dive — serious effort; this is the part the user cares about most. Do it carefully and completely, not quickly.
Read R/state/dossier.json. "rules" gives the rules for today's choices; "matches" lists the matches to research, sorted by "priority" (1 = legs of today's betje as the model proposes it, and on a day with European cup matches every CL, EL and ECL match of today that the model knows (roles "kandidaat CL-avond", "kandidaat EL-avond", "kandidaat ECL-avond" and the model's "CL-avond ...", "EL-avond ...", "ECL-avond ...", "Safe combi EL + ECL" combis), 2 = legs of the weekly superbet / second chance played today or tomorrow, 3 = other candidates for today's betje, 4 = weekly legs later this week, listed only when the weekly bet was made today). Each match has the model's numbers: form_last5 (W/G/V, recentste rechts), team_stats_last10 (goals, cards = yellow + red, corners, shots per match, for and against) and "markets" (per market: chance p, competition average, signal, odd with its source, the bookmakers' chance and "why": the streaks behind it).
Research every match with "research_due": true and priority 1, 2 or 3, and the priority-4 matches too when rules.week_made_today is true; at most 16 matches. Use the Agent tool to research matches in parallel (at most 5 subagents at a time, one match each); give each subagent the full JSON of its match from the dossier, TODAY, its output path R/research/<number>.json (mkdir -p R/research) and all the research instructions and the output format below, and tell it to write only that file. If the Agent tool is not available, research the matches yourself, one after the other.
Research per match:
- At least 5 WebSearch queries (in English and in the language of the league's country) and read at least 3 relevant pages with WebFetch. News must be recent (team news, injuries and suspensions, expected line-ups, press conferences: the last 7 days); note the date of what you use.
- Cover: form in all competitions (last 5–6 results, including cups and European matches, with scores; a midweek European match means rotation or tired legs); head-to-head (last meetings); absences (injured, suspended or doubtful players, especially key players and goalkeepers; confirm each with an official source or a second reliable source, else write "niet bevestigd"); expected line-up and rotation; the referee if appointed, with his cards per match; team discipline (cards per match this season — a team that collects many cards, like Getafe, matters for card bets); corner and shot tendencies when relevant; context (table position, what is at stake, derby, coach change, fixture congestion, travel, extreme weather).
- Sources: every source is allowed (the user said so). For facts that decide a bet (injuries, suspensions, line-ups, results, the referee) prefer official club, league and federation sites, major sports media (for example BBC Sport, Sky Sports, ESPN, The Athletic, The Guardian, Reuters, AP, Kicker, Sport1, L'Équipe, RMC Sport, Gazzetta dello Sport, Corriere dello Sport, Marca, AS, Mundo Deportivo, Relevo, A Bola, Record, O Jogo, Sporza, HLN, Het Nieuwsblad, Voetbalkrant, Walfoot, NOS, Voetbal International, AD, Fanatik, NTV Spor, BBC Scotland, Daily Record, MLSsoccer.com) and statistics and injury sites (FBref, Transfermarkt, SofaScore, FotMob, WhoScored, Soccerway, worldfootball.net, Premier Injuries, PhysioRoom), and confirm them with a second source when they come from elsewhere; previews, tipster and betting sites may be used for extra context, but say so when a fact rests only on such a site. If a site is blocked or fails, use another one; never work around a block. Do not invent anything: if something cannot be found, say so.
- Then judge the markets of the dossier for this match (at least every market with p of 0.55 or more, plus btts, bttsno and the card markets): verdict "sterk", "ok", "riskant" or "afgeraden", with one or two sentences that combine the model's numbers with what you found. Reason about every kind of bet: result, goals (over/under), BTTS, corners, cards (team discipline plus the referee), shots.
Output per match, R/research/<number>.json, Dutch text, no markdown: {"key": exactly the key from the dossier, "checked": "TODAY HH:MM" (current local time), "summary": 3 to 6 sentences on what matters most for betting on this match, "form": ..., "h2h": ..., "absences": {"home": ["Naam (reden, status, bron)", ...], "away": [...]}, "referee": "naam, kaarten per match" or null, "cards": 1-2 sentences on discipline and cards, "btts": 1 sentence, "context": ..., "markets": [{"id": market id from the dossier, "verdict": ..., "reason": ...}, ...], "best": [up to 3 market ids, strongest first], "sources": [{"title": ..., "url": ...}, ...] (at least 4 distinct reliable sources you actually read), "limited": true if you could read fewer than 3 relevant pages in full (for example because WebFetch failed), else false}. The finalize step sets "checked" to the time of the run itself. Limited research is redone at the next run and should not carry a pick on its own.
If WebFetch fails for a site, try other reliable sites (several outlets usually cover the same team news); if WebFetch fails for every site, say so in the summary and set "limited": true.

- "el" and "ecl": the same as "cl", for the Europa League-avond (today's EL matches only, roles "kandidaat EL-avond") and the Conference League-avond (today's ECL matches only, roles "kandidaat ECL-avond"), on every day with matches of that competition: 1 to 4 combis of 5x+ (at most 40x) each, 2 to 6 legs, the same spread rule within that competition's combis. - "eusafe": only on a day with both Europa League and Conference League matches: exactly 1 "safe" combi (the user wants one combined EL + ECL combi you see as safe), 2 to 4 legs from today's EL and ECL matches together with at least one of each, product of the dossier odds 1.8 to 3.5 (aim for about 2.5), the bets you are surest of (preferably rated "sterk", never "riskant" or "afgeraden"), score-settled markets only. - BTTS ("btts" = beide teams scoren: ja, "bttsno" = nee) is an ordinary market, like 1X or over 2.5: pick it as a leg of the betje (or of the weekly superbet on the day it is made) when your research makes a specific BTTS bet good.
